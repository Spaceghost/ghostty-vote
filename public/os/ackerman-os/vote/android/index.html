Privacy. GitHub or XIVAuth sign-in gives each account one ballot per Ackerman project. Votes, notes and suggestions are associated with a hash of the provider and account ID; that hash is not anonymous to the site owner. The server does not store your email, IP address or sign-in token. A signed session cookie lasts up to 30 days and ends at sign-out or 90 days after sign-in.

No FFXIV character is needed for Ackerman voting. A character linked elsewhere through the shared XIVAuth sign-in may still be visible to the owner. Notes and suggestions are visible only to you and the maintainer. Your browser keeps a copy of the ballot so choices appear immediately; public tallies can lag by a minute.
