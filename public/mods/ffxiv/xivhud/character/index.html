An adventurer’s record

Four pages show your equipped gear, attributes, classes and jobs, and saved gear sets. Equipment includes materia, condition, glamour and dyes. The record uses the installed game’s artwork and fonts.

Open the familiar way

Use the game’s Character action, the optional Umbra toolbar or /xivcharacter open. /xivcharacter native opens the original and /xivcharacter screen recovers the ordinary window. Native frames and Ghostty hosting are optional.
