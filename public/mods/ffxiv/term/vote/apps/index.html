Connected apps

The plugins and the command line cannot sign in with a browser, so they borrow your sign-in: the app shows a short code, you approve it here, and it may then share screenshots or submit benchmark results in your name until you disconnect it.

Sign in first

Everything new on this site comes from a signed-in GitHub or XIVAuth account. Your code is kept while you sign in.

    Your connected apps

    Privacy. A link stores which app it is, what it may do, when it was made, last used and when it ends (180 days), under the same account key as your ballot. The app's token is shown to the app once; the server keeps only a SHA-256 of it. Disconnecting an app here ends its access at once; what it already sent stays until you ask the owner to remove it through github.com/Spaceghost.
