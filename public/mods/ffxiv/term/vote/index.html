What should the terminal do in Eorzea next?

Ghostty for FFXIV is a plugin that puts a real Ghostty terminal inside Final Fantasy XIV, made by Johnneylee Jack Rollins (github.com/Spaceghost). These are ideas for what it could do next. Each was checked against how the plugin actually works: how feasible it is, how much effort it takes, and whether it stays client-side and within the game's terms of service.

Sign in, then mark each idea Want, Maybe or Skip, leave a note, or suggest something the list missed. The tallies are public and help decide what gets built next. None of this is a promise that an idea will ship.

Privacy. Voting needs a GitHub or FFXIV (XIVAuth) sign-in, so each account has one ballot. Your votes, notes and suggestions are linked to your GitHub or XIVAuth account id and are visible to the site owner. They are stored under a SHA-256 hash of the provider name and that id, and the hash does not hide who you are: GitHub account ids are public, so anyone who has the id can work out the hash, and the owner can look the account up. The server does not store your GitHub username, your email address, IP addresses or the sign-in token, which is used once and thrown away. The sign-in itself is a signed cookie that only this page can use; it lasts 30 days and renews while you keep visiting, but ends 90 days after you signed in, or when you sign out.

If you link a character, the site keeps the character you chose on XIVAuth: its name, world, Lodestone ID and portrait link, and when it was first and last seen. Only the site owner sees it, next to your ballot. Forget my character deletes it, and your votes stay. Notes and suggestions are visible only to you and the maintainer. A copy of your ballot is kept in this browser's local storage so the page can show it straight away. The tallies can be up to a minute old. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and character portraits from the Lodestone.
