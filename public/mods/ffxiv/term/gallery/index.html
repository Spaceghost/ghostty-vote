In game: with a Ghostty terminal on screen, take a normal screenshot with the game's screenshot key. The plugin asks Share to the Ghostty gallery?; click Share. /term share offers your latest screenshot at any time.

Sharing uploads the image to this site. It is shown publicly here, with the credit you gave, once the site owner has reviewed it. Location and camera details inside the file are removed before it is stored.

Privacy. Uploads need no account. The server keeps the image (with its embedded metadata removed), the optional credit, the image size and the upload time. It does not keep your IP address: a keyed digest of it counts uploads for the rate limit and is cleared once the screenshot is reviewed. Only screenshots the owner approved are listed or served. To have one taken down, contact the owner through github.com/Spaceghost.
