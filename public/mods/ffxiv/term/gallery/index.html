The plugin asks Share to the Ghostty gallery?; click Share. /term share offers your latest screenshot at any time. The first time, Ghostty (0.3.0 or newer) shows a short code and opens the connected apps page, where you sign in and approve it.

Privacy. Sharing needs a sign-in with GitHub or FFXIV (XIVAuth), on this page or through the plugin's link. With each screenshot the server keeps the image (with its embedded metadata removed), the optional credit, the image size, the upload time, which sign-in you used, a key derived from that account's id, and which linked app sent it, if one did. If you linked a character on the vote page, the site owner sees its name and world beside your screenshots. Only the credit is ever shown publicly. No login name, e-mail or provider token is kept. It does not keep your IP address: a keyed digest of it counts uploads for the rate limit and is cleared once the screenshot is reviewed. Only screenshots the owner approved are listed or served. To have one taken down, contact the owner through github.com/Spaceghost.

This page is counted, without cookies and without anything that identifies you: what this site counts.
