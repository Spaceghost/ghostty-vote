      Your history, your controls

      Only this signed-in account can read or export its history. XivPiano sends song, artist, album, duration and playback timestamps — no Pandora password, account token or stream URL. Saving and uploading recordings is a separate opt-in: only Upload & share sends MP3 audio. Hosted audio stays private until you create a listening link. Client tokens are stored as hashes; provider credentials are encrypted. Forwarding sends song metadata only to destinations you connect.

      History is kept for up to 365 days, with a limit of 100,000 listens per account. Export or delete history if you reach that limit. Deleting history also removes waiting forwards; already forwarded listens remain on the destination service. Revoking a player token stops new submissions from that player. Disconnecting a destination clears its credentials and cancels its waiting forwards. Site privacy
